1 Information We Collect

EcoImpact minimizes data collection to only what is strictly necessary to calculate habit points, calculate cumulative CO₂ metrics, and facilitate optional community leaderboard rankings.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Display Name: A nickname or alias you choose for yourself in your profile (e.g., “EcoAdventurer”). This name is visible to others only if you opt in to leaderboard sharing.
  • City & Country: Manually entered text describing your general locality (defaulting to “Local”). This is purely free-form text entered by you; we never access device GPS or location hardware.
  • Habit Completions: Specific eco-friendly actions you log in the app (e.g., reusable water bottle, cold-water laundry, plant-based meal, public transit commute), along with the date and time of completion.
  • Preferences: Your selected unit system (Metric: km/kg vs. Imperial: mi/lbs), theme mode (System/Light/Dark), notification reminder preferences, and leaderboard visibility toggle.

B. Automatically Generated Identifiers

  • Device Identifier (UUID): A randomly generated Version-4 UUID created locally on your device when the app is first installed (stored in private app DataStore). It is not linked to your device hardware, IMEI, MAC address, or Android Advertising ID.
  • Cryptographic Deletion Token: A 256-bit cryptographically secure random token (64-character hexadecimal string) generated locally on your device. The raw token remains on your device; only a one-way cryptographic SHA-256 hash of this token is transmitted to the server upon initial registration to verify and authorize future account deletion requests without requiring passwords.
  • Anonymous Authentication Session: When online synchronization is active, an anonymous account session identifier is provisioned via Supabase Auth (signInAnonymously()). This provides cryptographic session tokens (JWT) to enforce database Row-Level Security (RLS) without requiring your name, email, or credentials.

3 How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Impact Calculation & Scoring: To calculate your estimated carbon emissions avoided (kg CO₂) and points earned based on published scientific emission factors.
  • Offline-First Persistence: To keep your activity history, streaks, and unlocked milestone badges accessible on your device even without an internet connection.
  • Cloud Synchronization (When Connected): To back up your habit logs and total score so your progress is preserved across app sessions.
  • Community Leaderboard: If and only if opted in, to display your display name, city, and points on the community cohort leaderboard.
  • Local Habit Reminders: If enabled, the app uses Android's local AlarmManager / notification scheduler to deliver periodic nudges to log your habits. These notifications are scheduled 100% locally on your device without push notification servers.

4 Data Storage & Infrastructure

Local On-Device Storage

EcoImpact operates with an offline-first architecture. Your profile details, habit completion logs, points ledger, and unlocked badges are stored in an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database on your device using Android Room (EcoImpactDatabase). Device credentials and deletion tokens are stored in Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences in private app-specific storage inaccessible to other installed applications.

Cloud Storage & Service Providers

When you have an active internet connection, EcoImpact connects to cloud infrastructure provided by:

  • Supabase Inc. — Provides our managed PostgreSQL database, authentication service, and serverless Edge Functions. Cloud infrastructure is hosted in enterprise-grade AWS data centers located in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) with end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.

We do not use any other cloud providers, data brokers, or third-party storage vendors.

5 Community Leaderboard & Privacy

EcoImpact includes an optional community leaderboard displaying user rankings, tiers, and points.

  • Opt-In by Default with Full Control: You can toggle leaderboard participation on or off at any time in the Profile > Privacy Settings menu.
  • Masked Identifiers: The leaderboard view (public.leaderboard_view) never exposes your underlying device UUID or internal account IDs. User identifiers are irreversibly hashed using SHA-256 before being displayed in leaderboard queries.
  • Immediate Opt-Out Effect: If you toggle leaderboard sharing off, your profile is immediately filtered out of all community leaderboard queries.

6 Data Retention & 12-Month Inactivity Purge

We believe data should not be kept longer than necessary. Our retention policy is enforced both logically and through server-side automation:

A. Active Accounts

Your habit logs and score are retained as long as you actively use the application. Every time you open the app, sync habits, or update your profile, your activity timestamp (last_synced_at) is refreshed.

B. Automated 12-Month Inactivity Purge

Verified Automated Retention Rule: In accordance with database maintenance routine public.purge_expired_profiles_12m(), if an account has no synchronization or logging activity for 12 consecutive months (and is outside any initial migration grace period), all associated cloud records—including user profile, habit logs, points ledgers, and authentication credentials—are automatically and permanently deleted from our cloud database.

7 Account & Data Deletion Instructions

You have full, unhindered control over your data. We provide both an instant self-service deletion mechanism directly inside the Android application and a manual email request path.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete all local and cloud data directly within the app at any time:

1 Open the EcoImpact App

Launch EcoImpact on your Android device and navigate to the Profile tab (bottom navigation bar).

2 Tap “Delete All My Data?”

Scroll down to the bottom of the Profile screen to find the Data Management & Privacy section.

3 Confirm Deletion

A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap the red “Delete My Data” button to execute the deletion.

Important Disclosure Regarding Pending Deletion: When you confirm deletion, the app dispatches an authenticated request to our secure delete-account Edge Function using your device's cryptographic deletion token. The server permanently purges your cloud profile, habit history, point ledgers, and authentication account.

If the device is offline or the server cannot complete the request immediately (e.g. temporary network outage), the app safely records a durable pending tombstone on your device and keeps local records intact until the cloud deletion is confirmed. As soon as network connectivity is restored, the deletion retries automatically, wipes the local database, and clears the tombstone.

Method 2: Manual Deletion Request via Email

If you have uninstalled the application, lost your device, or are unable to use the in-app deletion button, you may submit a manual deletion request by contacting our official support email:

Email support.ecoimpact@gmail.com

Note: Because EcoImpact uses anonymous device identifiers without collecting your personal email address or passwords, please mention any known details (such as your display name or approximate date of registration) when writing to us so we can locate and remove any lingering cloud records.

8 Security Safeguards & Architecture

EcoImpact incorporates defense-in-depth security principles across the client and cloud architecture:

  • Cryptographic Proof of Ownership: Account deletion requires a 256-bit deletion token generated locally on-device. The backend validates the token using SHA-256 against an isolated database schema (private.profile_security) that is strictly inaccessible to public queries or anonymous PostgREST endpoints.
  • Zero Service-Role Key Exposure: Privileged administrative database keys (service-role keys) are never bundled into the Android application or transmitted over the client network. Privileged operations execute solely within secure, isolated server-side Edge Functions.
  • PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS): All database tables enforce strict least-privilege RLS policies. A user can only select, insert, or update their own authentic records (auth.uid() = id). Permissive legacy access policies have been completely dropped.
  • Anti-Cheat Server Validation: Habit completion points, carbon savings, and logging dates are checked by server-side triggers against canonical thresholds to prevent arbitrary score manipulation.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Android application and Supabase APIs occur over HTTPS using TLS 1.3.

Technical Limitations

Because EcoImpact relies on anonymous authentication stored in local device preferences, if you uninstall the app or clear app data while offline without internet access, your local device session is erased and cannot be recovered.

9 Your Legal Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Union under GDPR or California under CCPA/CPRA), you have specific statutory privacy rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You can view all your logged habits, streaks, impact calculations, and profile details directly within the app at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit your display name, city, country, and preferences directly on the Profile screen.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): You have the right to request permanent deletion of all your personal data using the in-app deletion button or by emailing support.
  • Right to Object & Restrict Processing: You can opt out of leaderboard participation at any time by toggling the leaderboard setting off.
  • Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against any user for exercising their privacy rights. All core habit tracking features remain fully functional regardless of your privacy choices.

10 Children's Privacy

EcoImpact is intended for general audiences interested in tracking environmental sustainability habits. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 where required by European Union law).

Because we do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or birthdates, we do not intentionally maintain identifiable profiles of children. If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has installed the application and wishes to have their activity data deleted, please contact us at support.ecoimpact@gmail.com and we will promptly remove any associated records.
