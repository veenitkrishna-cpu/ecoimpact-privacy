EcoImpact Privacy Policy

Transparent, offline-first personal carbon and habit tracking.

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 • Release Scope: Shipped Release v1.0.0 & Target v2.1 Architecture • Package: com.ecoimpact.app

Welcome to EcoImpact. Your privacy and trust are paramount to us. EcoImpact is engineered as an offline-first sustainability companion designed to help you track daily eco-friendly habits and estimate your avoided carbon footprint. We believe personal environmental action should never require invasive personal surveillance.

Release Version & Deployment Scope Notice: This document accurately reflects our current privacy practices while transparently distinguishing between:
  • Currently Shipped Release (v1.0.0): The build currently distributed as a signed release bundle. It operates primarily as an offline-first tool using device-generated identifiers and baseline cloud synchronization.
  • Upcoming Target Release (v2.1 Architecture): Introduces anonymous Supabase authentication, anti-cheat scoring validations, cryptographic deletion tokens, and strict database Row-Level Security. These enhanced protections apply only after the v2.1 client update and corresponding cloud migrations are deployed and operational in hosted infrastructure.

No Advertising or Trackers

Zero third-party advertisement SDKs, commercial trackers, or profiling networks.

No Location Permissions

GPS and network location permissions are never requested. Locality text is purely optional user input.

Anonymous Architecture

No email, password, phone number, or social media login required to track personal habits.

Data Deletion Options

In-app tool to reset local data and request cloud profile removal; enhanced server deletion is planned for v2.1.

1 Information We Collect

EcoImpact minimizes data collection to only what is strictly necessary to calculate habit points, calculate cumulative CO₂ metrics, and facilitate community leaderboard rankings.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Display Name: A nickname or alias you choose for yourself in your profile (e.g., “EcoAdventurer”). This name is visible on the community leaderboard unless you choose to opt out of leaderboard sharing.
  • City & Country: Manually entered text describing your general locality (defaulting to “Local”). This is purely free-form text entered by you; we never access device GPS or location hardware.
  • Habit Completions: Specific eco-friendly actions you log in the app (e.g., reusable water bottle, cold-water laundry, plant-based meal, public transit commute), along with the date and time of completion.
  • Preferences: Your selected unit system (Metric: km/kg vs. Imperial: mi/lbs), theme mode (System/Light/Dark), notification reminder preferences, and leaderboard visibility toggle.

B. Device & System Identifiers

  • Device Identifier (UUID): A randomly generated Version-4 UUID created locally on your device when the app is first installed (stored in private app DataStore). It is not linked to your device hardware, IMEI, MAC address, or Android Advertising ID.
  • Cryptographic Deletion Token (Planned v2.1 Architecture): In the local v2.1 codebase, a 256-bit cryptographically secure random token (64-character hexadecimal string) is generated locally on your device. The raw token remains on your device; only a one-way cryptographic SHA-256 hash of this token is transmitted to the server upon initial registration to verify and authorize future account deletion requests without requiring passwords. Note: The currently shipped v1.0.0 release does not generate or transmit cryptographic deletion tokens.
  • Anonymous Authentication Session (Planned v2.1 Architecture): When online synchronization is active in the upcoming v2.1 build, an anonymous account session identifier is provisioned via Supabase Auth (signInAnonymously()). This provides cryptographic session tokens (JWT) to enforce database Row-Level Security (RLS) without requiring your name, email, or credentials. In the currently shipped v1.0.0 release, requests connect without an active Supabase Auth session token.

3 How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Impact Calculation & Scoring: To calculate your estimated carbon emissions avoided (kg CO₂) and points earned based on published scientific emission factors.
  • Offline-First Persistence: To keep your activity history, streaks, and unlocked milestone badges accessible on your device even without an internet connection.
  • Cloud Synchronization (When Connected): To back up your habit logs and total score so your progress is preserved across app sessions.
  • Community Leaderboard: To display your display name, city, and points on the community cohort leaderboard (enabled by default; you can opt out at any time in Privacy Settings).
  • Local Habit Reminders: If enabled, the app uses Android's local AlarmManager / notification scheduler to deliver periodic nudges to log your habits. These notifications are scheduled 100% locally on your device without push notification servers.

4 Data Storage & Infrastructure

Local On-Device Storage

EcoImpact operates with an offline-first architecture. Your profile details, habit completion logs, points ledger, and unlocked badges are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database managed by Android Room (EcoImpactDatabase). This database resides in your device's internal app-specific private directory and is protected by the Android application sandbox (isolated Linux UID) and device-level operating system encryption where supported by your device hardware. EcoImpact does not install custom database-level encryption (such as SQLCipher). Device credentials and deletion tokens are stored in Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences in private, app-specific storage inaccessible to other installed applications.

Cloud Storage & Service Providers

When you have an active internet connection, EcoImpact connects to cloud infrastructure provided by:

  • Supabase Inc. — Provides managed PostgreSQL cloud database, authentication, and serverless Edge Function infrastructure. Network communications between the Android application and Supabase endpoints occur over encrypted HTTPS connections using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3, depending on client device operating system and network negotiation). Cloud infrastructure is hosted through Supabase's managed platform.

We do not use any other cloud providers, data brokers, or third-party storage vendors.

5 Community Leaderboard & Privacy

EcoImpact includes a community leaderboard displaying user rankings, tiers, and points.

  • Leaderboard Settings & Default Behavior: In the application's profile data model, leaderboard participation defaults to enabled upon initial profile setup (privacyShareInLeaderboard = true). You can turn leaderboard sharing off at any time in the app's Profile > Settings menu.
  • Opt-Out Design vs. Hosted Verification: The app is designed to stop sharing your profile through its leaderboard experience when this setting is disabled. However, the exact effect on hosted leaderboard data depends on the deployed backend configuration, which has not yet been fully verified in the deployed environment for the target v2.1 release.
  • Masked Identifiers (Planned v2.1 Architecture): In the baseline database schema definition (schema.sql), the leaderboard view references underlying account UUIDs directly. The planned v2.1 migration changes the view to mask identifiers using a SHA-256 digest. However, external Data API accessibility in the current hosted environment remains unconfirmed; the production dashboard previously reported zero exposed tables and zero exposed functions. We have not verified public reachability through an end-to-end request.

6 Data Retention Policy & Implementation Status

We believe user data should not be retained longer than necessary. Our retention objectives and current operational status are outlined below:

A. Active Accounts

Your habit logs and score are retained as long as you actively use the application. In both the current and planned architecture, performing an online synchronization refreshes your activity timestamp (last_synced_at).

B. Intended 12-Month Inactivity Retention Policy

EcoImpact's intended data lifecycle policy is to retain cloud records for no longer than 12 consecutive months after your last recorded synchronization activity.

Operational Implementation Notice & Limitations: While a database cleanup routine (public.purge_expired_profiles_12m()) has been authored in our local v2.1 database migration code, no automated background purge, scheduled cron job (e.g. pg_cron), or recurring manual maintenance workflow is currently active or verified in the deployed production environment.

Consequently, automatic or scheduled purging of inactive cloud records after 12 months is not currently operational or guaranteed. Deploying, scheduling, and verifying an operational retention purge mechanism remains an outstanding operational requirement before this retention policy can be automatically enforced.

7 Account & Data Deletion Instructions

You have full, unhindered control over your data. We provide both an instant self-service deletion mechanism directly inside the Android application and a manual email request path.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete your local data and request cloud record removal directly within the app at any time:

1 Open the EcoImpact App

Launch EcoImpact on your Android device and navigate to the Profile tab (bottom navigation bar).

2 Tap “Delete All My Data?”

Scroll down to the bottom of the Profile screen to find the Data Management & Privacy section.

3 Confirm Deletion

A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap the red “Delete My Data” button to execute the deletion.

Important Disclosure Regarding Deletion Architecture & Pending Deployment: Release Version Status: In the currently shipped v1.0.0 release, in-app deletion executes basic local database clearing and calls legacy database routines. The hardened deletion flow—which routes requests to a dedicated delete-account Supabase Edge Function to delete both database records and authentication accounts—exists only in local v2.1 code. It has NOT been deployed to or verified in hosted staging or production infrastructure. Therefore, complete end-to-end cloud account deletion cannot be guaranteed until the backend Edge Function and migrations are fully deployed, operational, and verified.

Offline & Retry Behavior (v2.1 Architecture): Under the v2.1 client implementation, if your device is offline or the server cannot complete the deletion request immediately (e.g., temporary network interruption or partial server response), the app records a durable pending tombstone in local preferences and preserves your local database records so no unconfirmed state is lost. As soon as network connectivity is restored and confirmed, the deletion request retries automatically, wipes the local database, and removes the tombstone.

Method 2: Manual Deletion Request via Email

If you have uninstalled the application, lost your device, or are unable to use the in-app deletion mechanism, you may submit a manual deletion request by contacting our official support email at support.ecoimpact@gmail.com.

Email support.ecoimpact@gmail.com

Identity Verification for Anonymous Accounts: Because EcoImpact uses anonymous device identifiers and does not collect verified email addresses, passwords, or phone numbers, display names are non-unique across the user base. To prevent accidental or malicious deletion of another user's records, manual deletion requests require sufficient corroborating information to verify account ownership (such as your device UUID if previously noted, your exact display name, city, approximate registration date, recent habit completions, or approximate points balance). If ownership cannot be reasonably verified from provided corroborating details, manual deletion cannot be executed, and the associated cloud records will remain in the database until an operational account-purging mechanism is deployed and executed.

8 Security Safeguards & Architecture: Current vs. Target

EcoImpact incorporates defense-in-depth principles across its client and cloud architecture. We distinguish between currently active safeguards and planned v2.1 upgrades:

A. Currently Active Safeguards (Shipped v1.0.0)

  • Transport Layer Security: All client-to-cloud communications occur over encrypted HTTPS channels using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3, subject to client device capability and server protocol negotiation).
  • Android Application Sandboxing: Local SQLite database and DataStore preferences are isolated by the Android OS using Linux UID separation and device-level operating system encryption where supported.
  • Zero Sensitive Key Bundling: Service-role / database administrator keys are not bundled in the client application.

B. Target v2.1 Release Architecture (Planned / Unverified in Hosted Infrastructure)

The following security enhancements are designed and implemented in local v2.1 source code and migration scripts, but have NOT yet been deployed or verified in hosted staging or production infrastructure:

  • PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS): The v2.1 database migration defines strict authentic tenancy policies where users can only select, insert, or update their own authentic records (auth.uid() = id), completely removing legacy permissive policies upon deployment.
  • Anti-Cheat Server Validation: Habit completion points, carbon savings, and logging dates will be checked by server-side triggers against canonical thresholds to prevent arbitrary score manipulation.
  • Cryptographic Proof of Ownership: Account deletion will require a 256-bit deletion token validated via SHA-256 against an isolated database schema (private.profile_security).
  • Zero Service-Role Key Exposure: Privileged administrative operations execute solely within secure, isolated server-side Edge Functions.

Technical Limitations

Because EcoImpact relies on anonymous authentication stored in local device preferences, if you uninstall the app or clear app data while offline without internet access, your local device session is erased and cannot be recovered.

9 Your Legal Rights & Limitations

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)), you may have specific statutory rights regarding personal data. Because EcoImpact processes data pseudonymously without collecting direct personal credentials (such as your legal name, email, or government ID), certain statutory rights apply to the extent that your account can be linked to your identity without disproportionate effort:

  • Right of Access: You can view all your logged habits, streaks, impact calculations, and profile details directly within the app at any time while the application is installed on your device.
  • Data Export & Portability Limitation: The EcoImpact application does not currently contain a built-in data export feature (such as an in-app button to export records in CSV, JSON, or other machine-readable formats). Furthermore, the project team does not currently have an established backend extraction tool or manual procedure to generate and deliver structured, machine-readable data exports upon request. While you may contact support.ecoimpact@gmail.com with general privacy questions, we cannot currently provide, promise, or guarantee the delivery of machine-readable data export files. Developing a verified data-export workflow or an in-app export feature remains planned future work.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit your display name, city, country, and preferences directly on the Profile screen at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): You have the right to request deletion of your data using the in-app deletion button or by emailing support (subject to identity verification limitations for anonymous accounts).
  • Right to Object & Restrict Processing: You can opt out of leaderboard participation at any time by toggling the leaderboard setting off in Settings.
  • Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against any user for exercising privacy choices. Core offline habit tracking features remain fully functional regardless of your privacy settings.
Notice Regarding Legal Compliance & Regulatory Review: Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)), data subjects may possess statutory rights to receive personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format. Disclosing current technical and operational limitations in this policy does not constitute statutory compliance or legal immunity. An independent legal and Google Play policy review remains required prior to commercial distribution.

10 Children's Privacy

EcoImpact is intended for general audiences interested in tracking daily environmental sustainability habits. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 where required by European Union law).

Because we do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or birthdates, we do not knowingly maintain identifiable profiles of children. If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has installed the application and wishes to have their activity data deleted, please contact us at support.ecoimpact@gmail.com with corroborating account details, and we will promptly assist in removing any associated cloud records.
